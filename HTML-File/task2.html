Chocolate Chip Cookies

Chocolate chip cookies are a classic and beloved treat enjoyed by people of all ages. They are soft or crispy cookies studded with sweet chocolate chips, offering a perfect balance of buttery dough and rich chocolate flavor. Easy to make yet endlessly satisfying, these cookies are often associated with home baking, comfort, and warmth. Whether served fresh from the oven with a glass of milk or packed for a snack on the go, chocolate chip cookies remain a timeless favorite in dessert culture around the world.

Preparation Time:
15-20 Minutes

Cooking Time:
10-12 Minutes per Batch

Servings:
About 24 Cookies (Depending on Size)

Ingredients you'll need

Dry Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 Cups (280 gram) all purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups (340 gram) semi-sweet chocolate chips

Wet Ingredients

  • 1 cup (230g) unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup (150g) granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup (165g) brown sugar, packed
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

Common Substitution

Butter

Can be replaced with margarine or coconut oil (1:1 ratio).
For a softer texture, use half butter and half vegetable oil.

Sugar

Granulated sugar → coconut sugar or cane sugar (1:1 ratio).
Brown sugar → white sugar + a teaspoon of molasses per cup

Egg

1 large egg → 1/4 cup unsweetened applesauce or 1 tablespoon ground flaxseed + 3 tablespoons water (let sit 5 minutes).

Flour

All-purpose flour → whole wheat flour (slightly denser cookies).
Gluten-free flour blend can be used 1:1 for gluten-free cookies.

Chocolate Chips

Semi-sweet → milk chocolate, dark chocolate, or white chocolate.
Chopped chocolate bars can replace chocolate chips.

Vanilla Extract

Can be substituted with almond extract (use half the amount) or maple syrup.

Cooking Instruction to Follow

  1. Preheat the oven: Set your oven to 375°F (190°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Mix wet ingredients: In a large bowl, beat the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar together until creamy. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each. Stir in the vanilla extract.
  3. Combine dry ingredients: In a separate bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour, baking soda, and salt.
  4. Mix wet and dry ingredients: Gradually add the dry ingredients into the wet mixture. Stir until just combined.
  5. Add chocolate chips: Fold in the chocolate chips evenly throughout the dough.
  6. Shape the cookies: Scoop tablespoon-sized portions of dough and place them on the prepared baking sheet, leaving some space between each cookie.
  7. Bake: Bake in the preheated oven for 10–12 minutes, or until the edges are lightly golden.
  8. Cool: Remove from the oven and let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 2–3 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
  9. Serve: Enjoy warm with milk or store in an airtight container for later.

Additional Tips for Chef

Here are some chef’s tips to make your chocolate chip cookies even better:

  • Use room temperature ingredients – Butter and eggs at room temperature mix more evenly and give a better texture.
  • Chill the dough – Refrigerating the dough for 30–60 minutes before baking helps prevent spreading and enhances flavor.
  • Don’t overmix – Once you add flour, mix just until combined to keep cookies soft and tender.
  • Choose your chocolate – Mix semi-sweet, dark, or even milk chocolate chunks for varied texture and taste.
  • Slightly underbake for softness – Cookies continue to cook slightly on the baking sheet after removal.
  • Add a pinch of salt – A little extra salt on top before baking enhances the chocolate flavor.
  • Experiment with add-ins – Nuts, oats, or dried fruit can add crunch and flavor.
  • Store properly – Keep in an airtight container at room temperature for up to a week, or freeze dough for later baking.
